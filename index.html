About Nan&Co

Nan means bread. We are a neighborhood bakery in Almaty with five branches, baking in a morning and an afternoon wave so shelves stay fresh and little is thrown away.

Whatever is left at the end of the day becomes croutons, garlic chips and bread pudding, so good bread keeps its value.
