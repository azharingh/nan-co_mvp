About Nan&Co

Nan means bread. We are a neighbourhood bakery in Almaty with five branches, and we bake in a morning and an afternoon wave so shelves stay fresh and little is thrown away.

Whatever is left at the end of the day becomes croutons, garlic chips, panini toasties and bread pudding, so good bread keeps its value.
